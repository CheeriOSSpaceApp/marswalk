MarsWalk

EVA traverse planner · Jezero Crater, Three Forks depot · NASA Space Apps 2026 · Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

Plans walking routes for astronauts on real HiRISE terrain, with oxygen as the cost. Every step is checked against one question: can the astronaut still get back to the habitat?

Opens the field tablet in a new window and the habitat console here. The tablet walks by itself (×300); the scenario injects its alert.

Demo

  1. Open the console and the tablet in two windows of the same browser.
  2. Console: click targets on the map, then Plan. The route appears on the tablet.
  3. Tablet: complete the egress task and press Walk.
  4. Console: inject an alert. The tablet receives the new route and the alarm.

Both screens talk through the browser (BroadcastChannel), so they must run in the same browser on the same computer.
