MarsWalk

Oxygen-aware EVA planner for astronauts at Jezero Crater · NASA Space Apps 2026 · Interplanetary Survival Guide: Martian Map

Routes on real HiRISE terrain with oxygen as the cost and "can I still get home?" checked at every step. The habitat console plans; the field tablet guides; alerts and obstacles re-plan in milliseconds.

🛰 Habitat consolePlan the EVA, inject alerts, watch the astronaut. 🧑‍🚀 Field tabletNext waypoint, O₂ vs. cost to get home, checklists, alerts. 🧪 Planner test benchClick two points, inject alerts, see the no-return ring.

Demo: open the console and the tablet side by side in the same browser. Click targets on the console map → Plan → on the tablet tick the egress checklist and press ▶ → back on the console press SPE → shelter: the tablet re-routes by itself.
